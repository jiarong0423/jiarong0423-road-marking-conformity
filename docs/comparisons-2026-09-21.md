# Every comparison this project ran, and which ones hold

2026-09-21. Reconstructed from the git log, `docs/`, `results/*.json` and
`results/figure_registry.json`. Nothing here is re-measured; every figure is
quoted as the record states it. Where a record and a commit message disagree
the disagreement is given, not resolved.

## The two lenses applied to every row

**The `carriageway()` defect.** `carriageway()` in `src/marking/extract.py`
computes the region markings are searched in. It judged "road" with a
roughness test, and a chevron is textured by its own stripes, so the test
called the chevron not-road and cut it out. Commit `47f08bf` (09-21 11:43)
and `docs/what-changed-2026-09-21.md`: on the one Street View frame showing
the chevron intact, "the marking mask and the region overlapped by 0.9%
where chance alone gives 9%", and "every threshold and score here was tuned
against 5% of the evidence". The fix inpaints the marking mask before
judging roughness: over 46 photographs it keeps **88.6%** of marking pixels
against **43.3%**, winning **46 of 46**, worst case 67.4%. Everything
measured before `47f08bf` was measured through the defect; the rows below
are marked `PRE-FIX` where that applies.

**The grid never included the original.** Every ensemble measurement is a
9-cell grid of 3 downscales × 3 JPEG qualities. The originals are
3072×4096 and were never measured. `docs/what-changed-2026-09-21.md`:
"Perturbation was supposed to be a check on a measurement. It became the
measurement, and the measurement was never taken." Rows are marked
`GRID-ONLY` where they rest on the grid.

## Conclusions resting on a comparison judged invalid

Listed with the reason, in rough order of how much of the project's argument they carry.

1. **"The taper measures 10.23° ± 0.55, a rate of 0.1806, an equivalent design speed of 29.3 km/h."** (row 14) — Four of six captures were taken as agreement while two were called outliers. `docs/reproducibility-2026-09-21.md` then showed the same quantity moving 11.5–14.0° on *one* photograph re-encoded, i.e. the whole spread of the "agreeing" four sits inside the method's own noise. Withdrawn in `results/taper_vanishing.json`. `PRE-FIX`.

2. **"Two routes with nothing in common land 1.2 degrees apart."** (row 15) — Presented as independent corroboration of 10.23° by the earlier 11.4°. The two routes share the extraction, the defective `carriageway()` region and the candidate-selection step, so they are not independent; and both arms are now withdrawn, with both registry entries moved to `withdrawn_figures` by `299a14e`.

3. **"The bridge's +5.9% grade probably explains the 18.82° and 2.61° outliers."** (`afd06b9`, row 14) — A hypothesis built to explain a residual that does not exist. `docs/reproducibility-2026-09-21.md`: "Both values sit inside the spread that compression alone produces on a single image… Closed, untested, because there is no longer an anomaly."

4. **"Canny+Hough is the best of the three detectors, so ELSED is not worth a Dockerfile change and an OpenCV 5 compatibility bet."** (row 34) — Void: run before the `carriageway()` fix, on input that had lost most of its signal, on 3 photographs, and generalised into a standing decision about untried detectors.

5. **"MSAC does not help — a negative result."** (row 42) — Void for the same reason. `47f08bf`: "A negative obtained on input stripped of its signal says nothing about the detector."

6. **"Linear parameter scaling makes it worse; the detector is scale-dependent and no single multiplier fixes it."** (rows 40–41) — The full-population run was well designed but pre-fix and therefore void. The pilot conclusion it replaced was itself invalid from 14 photographs at 2 qualities against a pipeline that uses 3.

7. **"M-LSD is measured the same way and used only if it wins."** (row 43) — `docs/opencv5.md` still presents this as the pending test. The outcome is recorded only in `docs/what-changed-2026-09-21.md`: it won on the grid, lost once letterboxed correctly, and is void either way as pre-fix. The two documents are inconsistent as they stand.

8. **"The only 2025-06 epoch survives; 2026-09's 24.9° is the stripes read as the edge."** (row 16) — Withdrawn: line groups were sorted into road and edge by angle window, which assumes the camera faced along the road. Street View headings were author-chosen and pavement photographs were not, so the arms differ in an uncontrolled factor.

9. **"The taper after the re-marking averages 0.186, near the 2025-06 figure."** (row 17) — "The average of eight bad numbers", with recovered pitch spanning −8.0° to +19.6°. Withdrawn; and the control it was compared against is withdrawn too.

10. **"The chevron band is about 1.9 m and all three captures agree."** (row 20) — Agreement manufactured by a degenerate affine rectification (horizon within 0.7 px of the stripes, warp scale spanning 462×). Withdrawn.

11. **"25% / 16% / 84.1% of the chevron was erased."** (row 19) — No negative class: the imprint test fired on roughly half of all non-paint pixels at every depth; the Gabor period returned at the clamp. Withdrawn.

12. **"64% of the chevron's width was painted out."** (row 18) — The measurement is sound in construction but rests on a hand-drawn edge whose ±20 px of plausible error spans 54.9%–68.3%, and on an unresolved confound between the chevron's former edge and the milling boundary. "About two thirds" survives; 64% does not. It is still quoted as 64% in the "Settled" table of `docs/status-2026-09-20.md`.

13. **"The tangent-to-chord change removed the lens dependence, 2.7× to 1.3×."** (row 11) — Cherry-picked: a measurable fourth capture (f40_p-35 at 0.042) was dropped from one arm. Like-for-like is 2.93× to 1.90×, and "no lens dependence left" is false at r = −0.997.

14. **"carriageway() separates noise from road cleanly."** (row 35) — Asserted from one noise image; over 24 it refuses 5 and accepts 19.

15. **"The guard band is ±0.018, set by this pipeline rather than by the painting."** (row 27) — The direction holds, but the number comes from 3 captures at one viewpoint, and `877f59a` shows n=3 self-consistency is a theorem about the sample size rather than evidence. Every live verdict the endpoint returned was decided by that band.

16. **"Publishing at 2000 px is lossless."** (row 48) — Asserted from 2 photographs; over 42 it changes 17 findings and 10 taper verdicts, five of them *gaining* `TAPER_TOO_STEEP`.

17. **"Degradation reads dramatically steeper."** (row 46) — Asserted from 4 photographs; over 22 it is +0.76° median and holds on only 12 of 22.

18. **"The carriageway was extended / the surface deteriorated / the kerb and drain covers were removed."** (row 52) — Three conclusions from comparing two different locations, then from reading absence into a crop twice. All withdrawn.

19. **"Eight aggregate counts prove the model cannot do the job."** (row 38) — The first reading was wrong (the input was starved), and the replacement comparison changes the representation and the option set together, so neither factor is isolated. Confidence is also not accuracy: none of these cases has a known correct label.

## Where a record and a commit message disagree

- **How much of the marking the broken region removed.** `a0eb294` (09-20 23:24): "a roughness test on the raw frame called it not-road and **removed 78% of the marking pixels**". `47f08bf` and `docs/what-changed-2026-09-21.md` (09-21): "**95%** of the evidence", and "from **5%** of the paint surviving to 98%". The 46-photograph measurement states it differently again: the median filter **keeps 43.3%**. All three are in the repository; none supersedes the others explicitly.
- **How many cells the reproducibility grid has.** `docs/reproducibility-2026-09-21.md` opens with "eight JPEG qualities (80-100) and three long sides" — 24 combinations — then reports every result as *x*/15, and `b98c69b` says "29 of 60 runs" over four photographs, i.e. 15 each. `6574530` says the ensemble "runs the frame at five encodings". The later ensemble is a 9-cell grid (3 × 3). The stated design and the reported denominators do not match.
- **The root cause of candidate selection.** `b98c69b`: "The rule takes the **last** group satisfying §171 rather than the strongest." Corrected in `docs/reproducibility-2026-09-21.md` and `6574530`: "it takes the **highest arms × lopsided**." The observation (3 members chosen over 30) stands in both.
- **The baseline taper rate.** The "Settled" table of `docs/status-2026-09-20.md` gives 0.202 and a 14.9 m taper; the §171 section of the same document gives 0.1806 and 16.6 m; `results/taper_rate.json` gives 0.202 → 27.8 km/h; `results/taper_vanishing.json` gives 0.1806 → 29.3 km/h; `a52ae60` gives 28.2 km/h; `27db888` gives 28 km/h.
