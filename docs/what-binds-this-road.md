# What actually binds a taper on this road

The gate returns WITHIN_REFERENCE and STEEPER_THAN_REFERENCE rather than
complies and does not comply, because the formula it uses is
施工之交通管制守則, written by 高速公路局 for 國道, and the site is a 縣道.
That was recorded as "applicability unresolved". It is now resolved, and the
answer is stronger than the caveat.

## The chain, as the road authority states it

新北市各區公所、新北市政府各機關或經本府路權移交機關道路挖掘作業審查原則,
版次 6.0, 104年12月, on the 交通維持計畫書:

> 計畫書內容應詳細說明道路挖掘施工中如何維持交通順暢及疏導周邊交通，降低對
> 交通之衝擊及避免事故產生，並應符合**新北市施工期間使用道路交通維持作業
> 規定**、**交通標誌標線號誌設置規則**及**道路交通維持有關法令**之規定設置

Three documents. Each was read.

| document | states a taper length? |
|---|---|
| 新北市施工期間使用道路交通維持作業規定 (101年發布, 102年修正) | **No.** Its only dimension is the threshold defining occupation of the road: 單方向未能留設三點五公尺以上空間供車輛通行 |
| 道路交通標誌標線號誌設置規則 | **No.** §171 fixes the chevron's geometry - 邊線 15 cm, 斜紋 20 cm, 間隔 30 cm, 斜四五度 - and says nothing about how long a taper must be |
| 道路挖掘作業審查原則 6.0 itself | **No.** It requires 設置車道縮減警示並派員指揮交通 and gives no geometry for it |

**施工之交通管制守則 is not in the chain.** It is 高公局's document for 國道
and nothing here adopts it.

## So the finding is an absence

No document binding work on this road says how long a lane-closure taper
must be. That is not a gap in my research; it is the state of the
regulation, and it matches what `docs/markings-regulation.md` already quoted
from 立法院 research - 「劃設標準並沒有明確、詳細的規範，導致各地的劃設標準
不一」.

It is worth noting that the same 審查原則 gives tolerances where it wants
them: 修復後路面以3米直規量測新舊銜接處，不得超過正負0.3公分. It specifies
flatness to 3 mm over 3 m. It does not specify the taper. The silence is a
choice, not an oversight.

## What this does to the gate

It vindicates the naming and sharpens the basis. The gate is measuring
against a reference that no law here imposes, and it should keep saying so -
but it can now say *why*, with the chain cited, instead of hedging that
applicability is unknown.

It also relocates the argument. A taper 14.9 m long that a driver cannot
cross in the 2.5 s they need to react is not a breach of anything, because
there is nothing to breach. The case is not that someone broke a rule. It is
that **the rule does not exist**, and a marking that fails every reaction-time
figure in the engineering literature was laid lawfully.

## Still open

Whether 市區道路及附屬工程設計標準 (內政部) or 交通部's 整體道路規劃指引
(114年2月) reaches this case. Neither is named by the 審查原則, but both are
national and might bind through 道路交通維持有關法令, which is a catch-all
that names nothing.
