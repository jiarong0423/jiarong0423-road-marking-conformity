# The claim: the works left a surface that is hard to ride

Stated by the person who rides this road. Unlike the taper, this one has a
standard that is **New Taipei's own and directly applicable** - it governs
road excavation works in New Taipei, which is what is happening here - and
the standard says how to measure itself.

## The clauses, verbatim

《新北市各區公所、新北市政府各機關或經本府路權移交機關道路挖掘作業審查
原則》, 版次 6.0, 104年12月. Full text at
`docs/source-ntpc-excavation-6.0.txt`.

> 瀝青混凝土面層修復完成後之路面應確實夯實滾壓並**與相鄰路面平順銜接**，
> 且路拱及坡度應依市區道路及附屬工程設計規範相關規定辦理。
> **新舊路面銜接處與原有路面之高低差，以3米直規量取單點高低差不得超過
> 正負0.6公分。**（於禁挖區域內辦理道路施工，修復後路面以3米直規量測新舊
> 銜接處，**不得超過正負0.3公分**）

> 各管線機構於道路設置人（手）孔、閥箱（含基座）等構造物延伸至路面之蓋板
> 時，**其頂面應固定與路面齊平、密合保持平順**，但不得封閉，並應隨時巡視
> 保持與路面平齊。**如與銜接路面產生高低差時，應即改善。**

> 施工完成後，**交接面須切齊平整密合並保持平順**

> 瀝青混凝土面層銑鋪厚度，不得少於五公分。

Four separate flatness requirements, one of them specifically about the
drain and manhole covers: their tops must be fixed flush with the road,
kept flush on inspection, and any height difference put right immediately.

## Why this claim is better founded than the taper one

| | taper | surface |
|---|---|---|
| standard | 施工之交通管制守則, 高公局, written for 國道 | 新北市's own, for excavation works in 新北市 |
| applies here | not established - 新北市's chain names no taper length at all | directly: this is a 新北市 excavation |
| numeric threshold | L = W·V²/155, but nothing binds it here | ±0.6 cm, ±0.3 cm in a no-dig zone |
| how to measure | not stated | **stated: a 3 m straightedge, single-point height difference** |

The taper work concluded that no document binding this road says how long
a taper must be. Here a document binding this road says exactly how flat
the surface must be and how to check it.

## What the photographs can and cannot carry

**Can**: that a joint between old and new surfacing runs along the
carriageway, where it runs, and how much of the running width it occupies.
A continuous band of broken and patched surface between the running lane
and the kerb appears in nearly every one of the 42 field photographs -
F05-F08, F17-F20, F22-F33, F37-F40 - so it is not a local defect but a
longitudinal strip along the works.

**Cannot**: the height difference. The threshold is 3 to 6 mm and no
photograph taken from standing height resolves that. Claiming otherwise
would be the same error as the withdrawn figures in `status-2026-09-20.md`.

**The standard supplies the instrument.** A 3 m straightedge laid across
the joint, and the single-point gap read under it, is the measurement the
clause names. It costs nothing and it is the one measurement in this whole
project that the person on the road can take and the imagery cannot.

## What would establish the claim

1. 3 m straightedge across the old/new joint at several stations, single
   point height difference each time, against ±0.6 cm.
2. The same across each drain and manhole cover, against 齊平.
3. Photographs of the straightedge in place with the gap visible, and the
   station recorded, so each reading has a location.
4. Whether this stretch is a 禁挖區域, which halves the tolerance to
   ±0.3 cm. Not yet checked.

Points 1 to 3 are field work. Point 4 is a records question and is open.

## Three more clauses, and one of them assigns the consequence

From the same 審查原則, found by searching it for what it says about the
condition a works leaves the road in:

> 交通秩序及環境清潔或**修復不良，而造成交通意外事故或國賠事件，概由管線
> 機構負責**。

The document does not have to be argued into contemplating that poor
restoration causes crashes. **It says so, and names who carries it.** The
causal step this claim needs is in the regulation, not in my reasoning.

> 道路挖掘施工路段標誌及號誌，應按交通維持計畫書規劃設置或遵照「道路交通
> 標誌標線號誌設置規則」有關規定設置，「**標誌力求鮮明顯目夜間加設閃光燈、
> 紅燈或貼反光紙**」完工後其安全設施始可撤除。

Which speaks to the night part of the claim: warning of the works must be
made conspicuous after dark, and the safety installations stay until the
work is finished.

> 如圍籬設置**7天以上暫無施工必要**者，除有特殊原因經路權機關核可者外，
> **應先行撤除圍籬並將路面予以修復**。

A test with a date attached. If the hoarding has stood a week with no work
needing it, it comes down and the surface is made good. Whether that
applies here is a question of observation - is anyone working - and the
rider is the one who can answer it.

## A foreign benchmark, for the milled face specifically

Work-zone guidance elsewhere puts a number on the condition in the
photographs - part milled, part filled, a step between:

> Milled, longitudinal or transverse vertical faces exceeding 25 mm in
> height that will be exposed to traffic during non-work hours shall be
> sloped or tapered by temporary patches or shims to avoid creating a
> traffic hazard.

25 mm, and specifically for a face left exposed outside working hours -
which is the night the rider is describing. It is not binding here and is
recorded as a benchmark, not as law. **The local clause at ±0.6 cm is
stricter and is binding**, so the foreign one is only useful for the case
of a face still awaiting its surface course, which the local clause
addresses as a finished state rather than a temporary one.

## One document that does not support this claim

交通部運輸研究所, 《混合車流情境之機車交通安全工程設計方法研究驗證與推廣》,
looked promising - Taiwan's own research on motorcycle safety engineering
in mixed traffic. It is about intersection layout. Searched for 高低差,
銑刨, 側滑, 路面不平, 坑洞: none appear. It does not speak to pavement
surface condition and is not cited for it.

## What the photographs can measure: how many surfaces the lane is made of

The height difference is out of reach, and two attempts to reach it are
worth recording because both failed for stated reasons.

**Shadow photometry.** A vertical step in oblique light casts a shadow, and
the shadow's width times the tangent of the sun's elevation is the step's
height - the method used for crater depths. The arithmetic here is
favourable: at 15:58 on 2026-09-20 at this latitude the sun is 26.3° up, so
a 6 mm step throws a 12.2 mm shadow, and with a lane dash or the red line
in frame for scale the ground sampling is about 5 mm/px, making that
2.4 px against a sub-pixel edge fit good to 0.1-0.3 px. Ten times the
margin needed.

**It fails on the light, not the resolution.** Measured on the field
photographs: road contrast is 138 levels, which is ample, but the
orientation coherence of strong dark edges is **0.19** on a scale where 1
is a single light direction and 0 is none. The day was overcast. There is
no shadow to measure, and no algorithm recovers a signal the photograph
does not contain. A photograph taken in direct low sun, with the sun
roughly across the joint and a marking of known size in frame, would carry
it.

**Counting discontinuities.** Long road-aligned brightness steps inside the
carriageway, before and after: 0, 11, 2 in 2025 and 9, 9, 17, 2, 0, 1 in
2026. The within-group scatter is larger than the difference between
groups, so it distinguishes nothing. Not used.

**What does work** is the question the eye is actually asking, which is not
"is there an edge" but "how many different surfaces is this". Clustering
each carriageway pixel by brightness and local texture, after the markings
are median-filtered away, and counting only clusters separated by more than
their own width and holding at least 12% of the lane:

| | surface types per capture | median |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-06, four captures | 1, 3, 2, 1 | **2** |
| 2026-09, seven captures | 5, 5, 4, 3, 3, 3, 5 | **4** |

**Half the 2025 captures show the running lane as a single surface. None of
the 2026 captures does**, and the fewest any of them shows is three.

That is not a height and does not by itself breach anything. What it
establishes is the premise the ±0.6 cm clause rests on: there are joints
between old and new surfacing in the running lane now, and there were
substantially fewer before. Where a joint is, the clause applies; how far
out of tolerance it is still needs the straightedge.

These are different viewpoints rather than a controlled repeat, so the
comparison carries the shift and not a precise ratio.
